## Introduction

SMSKart Manager ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our SMS management application.

## Summary

**SMSKart Manager processes all data locally on your device. We do NOT collect, store, or share your personal information with anyone.**

---

## Information We Access

SMSKart Manager accesses the following data on your device:

### 1. SMS Messages
- **Purpose:** Display, organize, and categorize your messages
- **Use:** OTP extraction, transaction detection, message categorization
- **Storage:** Local device only

### 2. Contacts
- **Purpose:** Identify senders and categorize personal messages
- **Use:** Display contact names for messages
- **Storage:** Local device only

### 3. Notification Access
- **Purpose:** Show new message notifications
- **Use:** Alert you about incoming SMS
- **Storage:** N/A

---

## How We Process Your Data

All data processing happens **entirely on your device**:

| Feature | Data Used | Processing |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Message Categorization | SMS content | On-device AI |
| OTP Detection | SMS content | Local pattern matching |
| Financial Tracking | Transaction SMS | Local parsing |
| Contact Identification | Contacts list | Local matching |

---

## Data We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do **NOT**:

- ❌ Send your messages to any server
- ❌ Share data with third parties
- ❌ Use advertising or analytics services
- ❌ Store your data in the cloud
- ❌ Access your data remotely

---

## Data Storage & Security

- All app data is stored locally using Android's secure storage
- Optional biometric authentication available
- Data encrypted using Android's built-in security
- No internet connection required for core functionality

---

## Your Rights & Control

You have complete control over your data:

- **View:** Access all your messages within the app
- **Delete:** Remove any message through the app
- **Export:** No cloud export - your data stays on device
- **Uninstall:** Completely removes all app data

---

## Permissions Explained

| Permission | Why We Need It |
|------------|----------------|
| `READ_SMS` | Display your messages in the app |
| `RECEIVE_SMS` | Receive and show new message notifications |
| `SEND_SMS` | Allow you to reply to messages |
| `READ_CONTACTS` | Show sender names instead of numbers |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Alert you about new messages and OTPs |

---

## Default SMS App Requirement

SMSKart Manager must be set as your default SMS app to function. This is required by Android to:
- Receive incoming SMS messages
- Send SMS messages
- Access your message history

---

## Children's Privacy

SMSKart Manager is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

---

## Third-Party Services

SMSKart Manager does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

**Optional Feature:** Google Drive backup (if enabled by user) uses Google's secure authentication. No message content is shared with Google - only encrypted backup data is stored in your personal Drive.
